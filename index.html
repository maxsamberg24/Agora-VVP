Before you start

How it works.

  • Once you hit enter, you will be shown Visual Value Propositions for multiple product ideas.
  • Some of them are programs, some are tools, some are apps, & some are even physical spaces. There are simple ideas, complicated ideas, & outlandish ideas. It is your job to vote on which ones are bad, which could potentially work, & which ones you would actually want to use.
  • Two quick questions per idea. Once you move on from an idea you can’t go back to it, so go with your gut.
  • Honest feedback highly encouraged. Without it I can’t make progress. I promise you won’t hurt my feelings.
  • Thanks for helping out. If you have any comments or ideas after, please feel free to reach out.
